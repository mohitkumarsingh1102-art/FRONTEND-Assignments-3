Event descriptions

Code Sprint

A 2-hour competitive programming contest. Solve algorithm and logic problems in Python, C++ or Java. Individual participation.


HackVerse 24-Hour Hackathon

Teams of up to four build a working prototype overnight around a surprise theme. Mentors are available throughout, and demos start at 4:00 PM on Day 2.


Robo Race

Build a line-following robot and race it on a timed obstacle track. Teams of two or three. Kits are not provided.


AI Quiz Bowl

A rapid-fire team quiz on artificial intelligence, machine learning, tech history and current trends. Teams of two.


Pixel Arena

A gaming tournament with knockout rounds on PC. The game list is announced on the registration page a week before the festival.


UI Showdown

Design a mobile app screen for a given problem in 90 minutes using Figma or any tool you like. Judged on usability and creativity.
