Welcome to TechFest 2027

About the festival

TechFest 2027 is the annual technology festival of Quad AI school of Technology and Management. For three days, students from colleges across the country come together to code, build robots, play games and learn from industry speakers. There are six competitions, hands-on workshops, a cultural night and prizes worth more than Rs. 2,00,000.
